## Completion summary

Outcome: Orbital surfaces now render end-to-end for ORCA, Gaussian-fchk, and Molden files. The async basis parse was split so the OpenQube BasisSet subclass (and its internal QFutureWatcher) is constructed on the GUI thread and only filled on the worker — fixing the silent queue-wedge where a basis constructed on a QtConcurrent pool thread orphaned its watcher, so the cube `finished()` was never delivered and no orbital surface ever rendered.

Changed: parseBasisFile split into constructBasis (GUI thread: chooses the GaussianSet/SlaterSet subclass) + fillBasis (worker: the stack reader fills it). loadBasis constructs the basis before dispatch and the worker no longer deletes the GUI-affine basis (the GUI-thread continuation does, on both the stale-epoch and fill-failure paths). The D2 watcher-affinity invariant is documented at the async entry points. End-to-end regression tests drive the real async path.

Evidence: Full ctest suite 44/44 under `xvfb-run -a` (exit 0); `orbitalselectiontest` 12/12; the affinity assert fails (`Compared QObject pointers are not the same`) and the end-to-end test times out (~89s wedge at `Cube calculation started.`) when the fix is reverted to construct-on-worker (recorded red runs), then both green with the fix. Tests: cmake --build build --parallel 8 → green. xvfb-run -a ctest --test-dir build --output-on-failure → 44 passed, 0 failed. QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen ./build/bin/orbitalselectiontest → 12 passed, 0 failed.
